About

Synthetic chemist with an engineer's eye for scale

I am a Ph.D. candidate in chemistry at the University of Houston (expected 2027), working in the May group on complex molecule synthesis and asymmetric catalysis. My work spans multi-step total synthesis, organometallic catalyst construction, and medicinal-chemistry side-projects, supported by air-/moisture-free Schlenk technique and full-spectrum analytical characterization. What I work on

Lead project

Chiral Dirhodium(II) Catalyst

Lead contributor on a 2nd-generation chiral bidentate Rh2(II) biscarboxylate catalyst (initial design by Dr. H. Barzegar) with α-quaternary stereocenters and an m-xylene tether bridging both Rh centers at ~90° bite angle for enantioselective carbene cascade reactions. Completed the 7-step ligand synthesis; currently assembling the metal complex.

Poster · TexSyn 2026
Medicinal chemistry

Warfarin & Flavone Scaffolds

Synthesized warfarin derivatives via R-TN1-organocatalyzed conjugate addition (ORP), demonstrating enantioselective organocatalysis on a medicinally relevant scaffold; produced a gram-scale flavone scaffold (~1.4 g × 4 batches) with an arylboronic-acid-driven analog series planned for biological-activity studies.

Polymer chemistry

Fluorinated AEM Candidates

Synthesized fluorinated poly(arylene piperidinium) hydroxide candidates aimed at anion-exchange-membrane applications for water electrolysis; investigated structure–viscosity relationships and characterized structure and end-group identity by NMR and MALDI-TOF.
